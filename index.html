Naveen Kumar Reddy Somireddy

Researcher in federated and continual learning · applying for PhD programs

I work on how learning systems adapt, retain and forget. Most of my research asks how a model can learn a sequence of tasks across many clients with different data without losing what it learned earlier, and how it can later be made to forget on request.

My approach is to treat forgetting as something to track and control. In FedQCL I did this with drift-plus-penalty optimization, and in DUET-CLU with a constrained primal-dual formulation. FedQCL was accepted as an oral at CoLLAs 2026, and DUET-CLU is under review at AISTATS 2027. I was a research assistant at IIIT Delhi with Prof. Ranjitha Prasad and a research intern at NIT Karnataka, Surathkal. More recently I worked as a data engineer at Tredence.

FedQCL: Federated Continual Learning via Drift-Plus-Penalty

Co-author · CoLLAs 2026 oral
  • Formulated federated continual learning as a stochastic control problem. Client-local virtual queues track accumulated forgetting, and one parameter balances stability and plasticity, with no gradient projection or extra communication.
  • Task-IL with ResNet-18 and five non-IID clients: 54.5% vs. 51.0% for DER++-FL on Split-CIFAR-100, 85.7% vs. 83.9% on Split-CIFAR-10, 33.4% vs. 31.0% on Split-TinyImageNet.
  • Class-IL on Split-CIFAR-100: 24.92% vs. 24.32% (near-IID) and 14.66% vs. 14.02% (non-IID) against DER++.
